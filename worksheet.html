Check the starting point.

Equal percentage changes do not cancel when they use different starting amounts. Start with 100, lose 50%, then gain 50%: you finish with 75.

A worked example

A 20% loss from 100 leaves 80. The missing 20 is one quarter of the remaining 80, so returning to 100 requires a 25% increase.

Required gain = loss ÷ (100 − loss) × 100.
